# Report style and build

The report uses the AAAI Press template that the course links:
https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/aaai-press-latex-template/jymjdgdpdmxp

`aaai.sty` and `aaai.bst` (the 1 January 2015 AAAI conference style) were downloaded unchanged from a public mirror of the template, with their original notices:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/quarbby/FYP-LaTeX-Template/master/aaai/aaai.sty
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/quarbby/FYP-LaTeX-Template/master/aaai/aaai.bst

From this directory, run `tectonic --keep-logs main.tex`, or pdfLaTeX, BibTeX and two more pdfLaTeX passes. The source selects OT1 encoding under XeTeX to keep the template's fonts, and handles PDF metadata for both engines. The report renders to eight US Letter pages.

Figures are generated by the scripts in `figures/` from the data in `figures/data/`, with the project's virtual environment:

- `make_pipeline.py` draws Figure 1 (`pipeline.pdf`).
- `make_results_figures.py` draws Figures 2 to 5 (`schedule.pdf`, `curves.pdf`, `confusion.pdf`, `errors.pdf`). Its `scatter_figure` is not used by the report.
- `make_error_analysis.py` writes `data/error_analysis.json` from the run checkpoints and draws Figure 6 (`hard_examples.pdf`).
